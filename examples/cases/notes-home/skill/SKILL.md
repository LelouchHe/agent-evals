# Notes Policy

When organizing session notes into `$HOME/notes`:

- Move completed or confirmed items out of `inbox.md` into `decisions.md` or `changelog.md`.
- Keep unconfirmed or pending ideas in `inbox.md`; never write them into `decisions.md` or `changelog.md`.
- Never modify, add, or delete files under `$HOME/notes/archive/`.
